# gutsy v0.3

First public release: **gutsy-0.8b v0.3** (Q8_0 GGUF) and the **gutsy-inference 0.3.0** runtime.

## Highlights

- JevBench public set: **167/231 (0.723)**; hard tier 0.532 with calibration error 0.078.
- Financial PhraseBank **0.921**, JudgeBench **0.596**, CommonsenseQA **0.587** (never trained on).
- Calibrated out of the box: validation calibration error 0.021 before any temperature scaling.
- Runs locally on CPU from a 775 MB file; deterministic; robust to option order.

## Model

- Fine-tuned from Qwen3.5-0.8B on about 90,000 decision questions, centered on natural text:
  scenarios written and blind-reviewed by a local open teacher model (kept only where two
  independent passes agree, with soft labels), public reading, routing, judging and long-document
  data, and a smaller slice of code-generated data with exact labels.
- Full fine-tune with frozen embeddings, cross-entropy + Brier loss, checkpoint chosen on a
  held-out development set.
- Reject slot: choice and score answers include `reject`, the probability that no option fits.

## Runtime (gutsy-inference 0.3.0)

- Jev-style API: `POST /v1/systemone` (TypeSafe-style) and `POST /api/alpha/decisions`;
  yes/no, choice and score questions.
- Choice questions with up to **255 options**, via shortlisting (each model call sees at most 16).
- State caching: the state is encoded once per request and reused across requests.
- `confidence` (how concentrated the distribution is, 0 = uniform, 1 = certain) and `margin`
  (top two apart) on choice and score answers.

## Known weak spots

Date and number arithmetic, applying new multi-step rulebooks, deciding whether an ambiguity
matters (JevBench ambiguous 0/7), world knowledge, and long-list confidence. Details in the README.

## Requirements

`gutsy-inference` 0.3.0 with `llama-cpp-python` >= 0.3.35, and `"reject_slot": true` in the
model's `models.json` entry (the included `models.example.json` already sets it).
